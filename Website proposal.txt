### What Makes This Demo Land Well in a Pitch

* **Visual Palette:** Use deep charcoal/black (`#111827`), warm gold/amber (`#D4AF37` / Tailwind `amber-500`) to echo *Cordon Doré*, and warm cream/white backgrounds—giving it an upscale bistro feel without losing warmth.
* **Mobile-First Proof:** When you show it to the owner, pull it up on your phone side-by-side with their current site. The difference in navigation, readability, and 1-tap calling will sell the redesign on its own.
